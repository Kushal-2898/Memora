1 Local-First Architecture & Data Minimisation

Memora is engineered under the principle of Privacy-by-Design. We believe that your personal documents, medical prescriptions, bills, insurance policies, and expiry dates belong strictly to you.

Guiding Commitment: Memora never sells, rents, licenses, or shares your personal data, document images, or expiry dates with advertisers or third-party data brokers. Zero advertising SDKs are embedded in the app.

A. Guest Mode (Default)

  • You can utilize 100% of core scanning, tracking, and local reminders without creating an account.
  • Zero personal identifiers (names, emails, phone numbers, passwords) are collected or required.
  • All extracted dates and images remain strictly confined to your device's sandboxed storage.

B. Connected Account Mode (Optional Google Sign-In)

  • If you choose to link a Google Account, Memora receives your basic Google profile (Display Name, Email Address, and Avatar URL) solely to authenticate your identity.
  • Account linking enables optional encrypted cloud backup and cross-device sync.

2 Hybrid On-Device & Optional Cloud AI Processing

When you capture photos of document labels, food items, medicine strips, or receipts:

  • On-Device Machine Learning (Default): Optical Character Recognition (OCR) is performed entirely on your device using Google ML Kit's local neural engine. Scanned photos never leave your device for standard date detection.
  • No Silent Background Transmissions: Photos and candidate text are never streamed to remote servers during standard capture.
  • Optional Cloud AI Refinement (Gemini): For difficult, low-contrast, or multi-date packaging, Memora provides an optional "Analyze with AI" feature. This is triggered strictly upon your manual, explicit tap on the scan review bottom sheet. Images are transmitted securely over encrypted TLS solely for date extraction and are never retained, sold, or used for model training.

4 Account Deletion & Data Erasure (Right to Erasure)

Under India's DPDP Act, 2023, EU GDPR, and Google Play Data Safety policies, you hold complete autonomy over your data.

How to Delete Your Account and Data:
  • In-App Deletion: Open Settings > Privacy & Data > Delete Account. Confirming instantly removes your Google account authentication, purges your session, and wipes cloud-associated backup records.
  • Erase Local Data: Open Settings > Privacy & Data > Erase All Data to permanently destroy all locally stored memories and images.
  • Web / Email Deletion Request: You can also request complete deletion of your account and any associated data without opening the app by emailing privacy@memora.app or klabsstudios@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request". Requests are honored within 48 hours.
