How Memora Works

Built for real-world packaging — fast, private, and reliable even without internet.

1

Point & Scan

Snap any product label, medicine blister, or bill. On-device ML Kit OCR and Gemini AI automatically separate MFG vs. EXP dates and filter out packaging noise.

2

Exact Reminders That Stick

Scheduled directly with Android AlarmManager so battery savers don't kill them — with 9:00 AM morning alerts, 6:00 PM "Due Today" follow-ups, and 3-hour intra-day snooze.

3

Private & Backed Up

Everything works offline-first on your device with secure Google account sync, zero advertising SDKs, and instant one-tap account and data erasure.
